ACCOUNT DELETION POLICY

How to request account deletion on SAMIR TOPUP, and what happens to your data.

1. How to Request Deletion

You can request account deletion through the support channels listed on our Contact Support page or directly via Telegram support. Include the email or phone number needed to identify your account.

2. What Gets Deleted

After verification, we remove your profile information and disconnect linked credentials. You will no longer be able to sign in after deletion.

3. What We Keep, and Why

Completed order and transaction records may be retained for a limited period where needed for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations.

4. Wallet Balance & Pending Orders

Resolve pending orders and withdraw or utilize any wallet balance before requesting deletion.
