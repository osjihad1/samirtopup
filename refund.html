REFUND & RETURN POLICY

This policy explains refunds and returns for digital products purchased from SAMIR TOPUP.

1. Digital Products

Top-ups, vouchers, and game credits are digital products. Once successfully delivered to the submitted account or player ID, they cannot normally be returned or refunded.

2. Failed or Cancelled Orders

If an order fails or is cancelled before delivery, the eligible amount will normally be returned to your site wallet or original payment method, depending on the payment process used.

3. Incorrect Information

We cannot refund a successfully delivered order when an incorrect player ID, server, region, or account detail was supplied by the customer. Please verify your UID before placing an order.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Delivery

If you believe an order was duplicated, charged incorrectly, or delivered incorrectly, contact support with the order ID and payment details so the issue can be reviewed.

5. Processing Time

Approved refunds may require several business days depending on the payment provider (bKash/Nagad). Wallet balance refunds are usually credited immediately.
